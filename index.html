About Me

Computational biologist with an M.Sc. in Biophysics and research experience in cancer genomics, transcriptomics, and deep learning. My research focuses on developing machine learning models for predicting radiotherapy response by integrating bulk and single-cell RNA sequencing data. In parallel with my research, I have gained hands-on experience in clinical whole-exome sequencing (WES) analysis, variant interpretation, and ACMG/AMP-based variant classification within a diagnostic genetics laboratory.

I am seeking PhD opportunities in computational biology, cancer genomics, precision oncology, and AI-driven biomedical research.
