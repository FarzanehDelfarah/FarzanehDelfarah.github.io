About Me

Computational biologist with an M.Sc. in Biophysics and research experience in cancer genomics, transcriptomics, and machine learning. My research focuses on developing deep learning methods for predicting radiotherapy response by integrating bulk and single-cell RNA sequencing data.

Alongside my academic research, I have gained extensive hands-on experience in clinical whole-exome sequencing (WES) analysis, variant interpretation, and ACMG/AMP-guided variant classification within a diagnostic genetics laboratory.

I am currently seeking Ph.D. opportunities in computational biology, cancer genomics, precision oncology, and AI-driven biomedical research.
